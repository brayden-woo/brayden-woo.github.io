About Me

Brayden Woo was born in Framingham, Massachusetts, and has lived a town over in Natick for all of his life. He completed his primary schooling at Natick High School in the spring of 2022, and is currently projected to graduate from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in the spring of 2026.

An avid reader for most of his life, Brayden has pursued a degree in English at UMass, and is considering looking to the editing/publishing industry for a future career. Outside of the great stresses of the job search, Brayden also hopes that he will be able to make more time to read for leisure once he graduates. A fan of creatively structured stories, some authors he has appreciated in the past include Kurt Vonnegut, and Adam Gidwitz.

Aside from reading, Brayden has also picked up (and dropped) a smattering of other creative hobbies, the longest enduring one being attempting to play the piano. He spends many a weekend haphazardly messing around the keyboard, practicing his meager repertoire, and, as of this year, drilling some scales. His most recently learned piece is a Fugue in D minor, BWV 1178, attributed to J.S. Bach in 2025.
