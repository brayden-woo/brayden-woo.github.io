About Me

Outside of the great stresses of the job search, Brayden also hopes that he will be able to make more time to read for leisure once he graduates. A fan of creatively structured stories, some authors he has appreciated in the past include Kurt Vonnegut, Haruko Ichikawa, and Adam Gidwitz.

Aside from reading, Brayden has also picked up (and dropped) a smattering of other creative hobbies, the longest enduring one being attempting to play the piano. He spends many a weekend haphazardly messing around the keyboard, practicing his small repertoire, and, as of this year, drilling some scales. His most recently learned piece is a Fugue in D minor, BWV 1178, attributed to J.S. Bach in 2025, and he is looking to start on BWV 878 after graduation.

Some other music Brayden enjoys listening to includes soundtracks to TV shows, movies, and video games, as well as general pieces under the "classical music" umbrella. Recently, he has started listening to some jazz fusion as well, like with musicians such as Pat Metheny and Pat Martino. He tends to be fairly out-of-the-loop when it comes to contemporary popular/pop-adjacent music.

Brayden is also currently starting to learn how to draw; he thinks that this extension across mediums may help him expand his general storytelling capacity, as he slowly works on personal creative writing. He has made little progress so far, but, like everything else, he hopes to have more time for regular practice after graduation.
