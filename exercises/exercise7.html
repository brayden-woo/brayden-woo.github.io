Louisiana vs. Callais Opinion Paper

A 2000~ word opinion paper written after discussing the Louisiana vs. Callais Supreme Court Case in class, and reading relevant legal briefs. This was my first time engaging with legal texts, and I believe that the end result was a well-informed, personal interpretation of the rhetorical strategies used by both parties in this case.
